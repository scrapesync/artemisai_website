Cross-functional rhythm

Every sprint also carries a Filza legal ticket, an Alex strategy ticket, and a Jill cost ticket on top of the engineering and product work. The launch path additionally depends on three research-driven workstreams folded into the relevant tickets below.

Facebook App Review / Live Mode S21, Filza

Business Verification is required for Advanced Access and must start early (June). App Review is required before any user without a role on the app can use it. Pilots run on dev-mode tokens and skip review until the Live-Mode flip. The screencast must be unique to the product and recorded on the working live site, not a mockup. Prerequisites in Settings > Basic: Privacy Policy URL, data-deletion callback, deauthorize callback, app icon, category.

Art-E RAG / Pinecone S16 to S18

One namespace per tenant for clean isolation and bulk delete on churn, plus a tenant_id metadata filter at query time as defence in depth (never namespace alone). Embeddings can be reversed to reconstruct source text, so encrypt at rest and treat the vector store as personal data. Use hybrid (sparse + dense) search. The citation gate makes the model judge sufficiency first and blocks any uncited number. Per-tenant cost guardrails: hard quota + circuit breaker, cache hot answers, stream tokens.

GDPR + multi-tenant isolation S19, S21, Filza

DSAR deadline is one month from receipt. Any "what do you hold on me" via any channel counts. Erasure must remove data from the DB, logs, backups, and third parties (Pinecone vectors, LLM provider logs), with a tested deletion workflow and proof kept for auditors. Isolation is a launch condition: tenant_id on every row, every query/cache-key/queue-message tenant-scoped, an alarm on cross-tenant access, and regular isolation tests. ICO registration required. Penalties up to EUR 20m or 4% of turnover.
