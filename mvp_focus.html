Alex's "Path to Simplicity" vs Current MVP Scope

Alex's note argues less features, more intelligence — get our first users genuine value with zero friction, and give them a reason to invite others. This page reconciles his core MVP against what's currently tagged MVP, shows the timeline impact, and lists the decisions for Asad (CTO), Alex (CEO) and Rafeh (PM) to make together.
Owners of this decision: Asad · Alex · Rafeh

1Alex's core MVP — and it already aligns

Art-E — grounded Q&AMVP ✓The explainer + predictor. Takes the stress off the user; no graph-reading required.
Alerts → act nowMVP ✓"This is happening" — positive (viral) / negative (brand risk). The watch-your-back promise.
Intelligence feedMVP ✓Audience, content, mood — "this is what happened, this is what to do".
Optimal timingMVP ✓When to post — explained by Art-E if needed.
Virality & predictionsMVP ✓Predicted score + reach before posting.
Discovery (preview)MVP ✓ nowView-only page + invite hook. Full matching/collab engine stays Phase-2.
Flow he drew — Connect (OAuth) → Artemis pipelines (OCR/NLP) → Art-E welcome → 2-year analysis → {Virality · Intelligence feed + Discovery · Alerts to phone} — is exactly the foundation→OAuth→Art-E chain we built. His five must-haves are all already MVP. The disagreement isn't direction — it's how much else rides alongside them.

4Two things Alex treats as MVP that we currently defer

  • "Direct to phone" alerts. Alex wants the alert pushed to the phone in the MVP. We have crisis detection as MVP but the push channel (email/SMS/WhatsApp) as Fast/Later. Decision: if "act now on your phone" is core, move at least email/push delivery into MVP (~3–4d).
  • Audience breakdowns. He implies audience analysis at launch; we have basic mood/content as MVP but audience segmentation & demographics as Later. Decision: agree how much audience depth is "MVP" — a light demographic snapshot vs full behavioural segments.

5Gamification — added, staged Later

Alex's variable-rewards thinking is now a tracked "Engagement & Rewards" category (5 features): achievement scores + mastery, social validation (star/save pages), variable reward feed, rewards-of-the-hunt, and Art-E suggestion completion/streaks — all LATER per the team call. One to revisit: Alex frames variable rewards as the invite-loop driver, so a light social-validation / starring mechanic might deserve to ride alongside the Discovery preview rather than wait.

⚑ Decisions for Asad · Alex · Rafeh

1. Focus the MVP? Adopt Alex's lean core (cut Drafts + heavy surface/settings work → ~114 tickets) to buy back ~7 weeks of buffer and keep Route 2 viable.
2. Push-to-phone: in MVP or fast-follow?
3. Audience depth: light demographic snapshot at launch, or keep full segmentation as Phase-2?
4. One gamification piece (social validation) alongside Discovery preview for the invite loop — yes or hold?
5. Hire decision: Route 1 (new AI engineer, +£1k/mo) gives margin even at fuller scope; Route 2 only works if we focus. Tie this to decision 1.
