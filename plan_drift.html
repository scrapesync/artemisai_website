The six drifts · proposed moves
D2
Graph API metric replacement lands in 3 days. No ticket exists.
Urgent · dated

The assessment says

"They removed impressions and page_fans (Nov 2025) and are replacing legacy reach / viewer metrics by June 15, 2026... retiring v19 in May 2026, v20 in Sept 2026. One routine policy change could break our product in a quarter."

The plan today

Zero tickets anywhere in the 530 mention the metric replacement or an API version audit. LOKI and the warehouse read the metrics being replaced on Sunday.

NOT IN PLAN→THIS WEEK

Proposed move

Raise an out-of-cadence ticket immediately, owner Faheem or Muteeb: migrate LOKI + RDL tables to the replacement reach / viewer metrics, pin and test Graph API v21+, and add a weekly deprecation-watch check to VD-INFRA-012 observability. Do not wait for a sprint boundary; the deadline is Meta's, not ours.

Alex's call
D1
"Get off Facebook-only" is the survival move. The plan is 100% Facebook.
Critical · missing

The assessment says

"Add Instagram (nearly free, same API), then TikTok / LinkedIn. This is the survival move, it kills the platform risk... we can't out-execute an API change, we can only diversify away from it."

The plan today

Across 218 features and 530 tickets there is not one Instagram, TikTok or LinkedIn ingestion item. Every byte we process arrives through the one API Meta is tightening.

NOT IN PLAN→FAST + LATER

Proposed move

Add 3 new PLAN features under Foundation & Data: "Instagram ingestion (Graph API)" as FAST (it reuses LOKI, the same tokens and largely the same schema, so it slots into FT-1 as VD-IG-001..003 without touching the Nov 28 build), plus "TikTok source" and "LinkedIn source" as LATER. Decision on whether IG groundwork starts before launch belongs to the runway call at the bottom of this page. No sprints renumbered.

Alex's call
D3
Agencies are the beachhead. Everything multi-page sits in Phase 2.
High

The assessment says

"Win agencies. They manage many pages, pay the most, and Meta's single-page tools ignore them. Our beachhead."

The plan today

"Multi-page / team accounts", "Invite teammate / seats" and RBAC are all LATER. The 5 pilots are single pages. The pilot expansion ticket VD-RAFEH-019C has no agency criterion.

LATER→FAST

Proposed move

Promote "Multi-page / team accounts" LATER → FAST (first post-launch build), point the 5 → 15 pilot expansion (VD-RAFEH-019C) explicitly at agencies, and add an agency page + pricing tier to the S22 marketing site ticket VD-LEGAL-WEB-022 scope. Multi-page connect is already half-built: the OAuth backend VD-OAUTH-BE-021 specifies a multi-page token model.

Alex's call
D4
"Build the MVP around cross-page." The MVP ships a view-only preview.
High · partial

The assessment says

"Cross-page intelligence = the moat. Meta shows your page; we show the whole neighbourhood. Build the MVP around this."

The plan today

Discovery is a view-only preview (VD-DISC-UI-013, MVP); the real engine is Phase 2. Partially aligned: the structural fingerprint already captures the moat's data from day one (VD-MLP-010, S10/S13, MVP). "Benchmark vs category" is FAST.

ENGINE LATER→TASTE OF IT IN FAST

Proposed move

Keep the engine Phase 2 (the date is real), but give users a taste of the neighbourhood at launch: promote "Benchmark vs category" to the front of FT-1, widen the Discovery preview copy in VD-DISC-UI-013 to tease cross-page comparisons, and make "your page vs the neighbourhood" the spine of the launch narrative in VD-STRAT-018MVP. The fingerprint capture stays exactly as planned; it is the quiet asset compounding under all of this.

Alex's call
D5
Collaboration should lead the pitch. The pitch currently leads with trust.
Medium · copy only

The assessment says

"Matchmaking is the thing Meta is least likely to copy and it's naturally viral. Make it the marketing hook and growth engine. Lead the pitch with collaboration; build retention on prediction + alerts."

The plan today

The locked one-sentence pitch (VD-STRAT-018MVP) leans on the trust story (cited Art-E + crisis). Collab matching itself is correctly LATER. Waitlist + referral mechanics are already MVP (S22).

BUILD UNCHANGED→COPY REWRITE

Proposed move

No build change. Rewrite the launch one-liner and S22 site copy to open with the collaboration / neighbourhood hook, keep prediction as proof and trust as the close. Touches VD-STRAT-018MVP and VD-RAFEH-021A only. Cheapest item on this page.

Alex's call
D6
Basic analytics just became free inside Facebook. Stop headlining it.
Medium · positioning

The assessment says

"Creator Assistant made plain-language analytics and what / when to post free inside Facebook. We can't win those. We stop competing on basic analytics."

The plan today

Best Times is MVP cut-order #1 (already the first thing to drop) and S6 positioning (VD-STRAT-006MVP) headlines image intelligence + best-times as proof points. That headline now describes a free Meta feature.

MVP · CUT #1→KEEP, DEMOTE IN STORY

Proposed move

Keep Best Times in the MVP as table stakes (cut #1 stands, and cutting it is now strategically cheap), but reframe it in-product as an input to prediction rather than a headline, and sweep S6/S7 positioning tickets so nothing we lead with is something Creator Assistant gives away free.
