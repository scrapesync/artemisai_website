Question 1 · the big one

Network theory in the MVP: what it costs, what we trade

The Phase 2 backlog holds 15 network tickets; deduped, that is 7 real epics. Two of them (graph-of-graphs via Node2Vec, global GraphSAGE) are research, not launch features, and stay in Phase 2 under any scenario. The rest convert to roughly 4 MVP-grade tickets per epic. Pick a scenario on the left, toggle cuts on the right, and the launch date recomputes from real velocity.

Question 2

Instagram pages → Fast-Track

Yes · zero MVP delay · one trick to do now

Build it in FT-1, the week after launch, as VD-IG-001..003: IG ingestion reuses LOKI, the same Graph API tokens and largely the same schema, so it is the cheapest platform we will ever add. TikTok and LinkedIn stay LATER.

The trick that must happen before launch: add instagram_basic + instagram_manage_insights to the scopes requested in the S19/S21 Meta App Review (VD-OAUTH-BE-021, one checklist line). Same review, no second cycle, and IG switches on in Fast-Track without going back to Meta. Miss this and FT-1 waits weeks on a fresh review.

Alex's call
Question 3

Do we need basic analytics in the MVP?

Keep it · it is only 9 tickets (~0.7 weeks)

Yes, but it is smaller than it sounds. The heavy lifting (pipeline, warehouse, NLP) sits in Sprints 1–5, already done. What remains tagged analytics in S6–S23 is 9 tickets across 7 sprints, mostly wiring existing warehouse data to already-designed pages: roughly 4% of the remaining build. Cutting it would save days, not weeks, and pilots would immediately compare us unfavourably to free Facebook insights.

The real change is positioning, not scope: analytics is the container predictions and insights live in, never the headline. Creator Assistant gives plain-language analytics away free; we keep parity quietly and sell what it cannot do, the neighbourhood.

Alex's call
