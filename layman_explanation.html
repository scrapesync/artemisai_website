What We're Building,
In Plain English

No jargon, no tech-speak. If someone asked you "so what does Artemis actually do?" at a dinner party — this page is the answer.

💡
The one-sentence version
Artemis is a smart assistant for people who run Facebook pages. It watches how their posts do, figures out what's working and what isn't, and simply tells them what to do next to grow — in language anyone can understand.
🍽️ Think of it like this: a personal trainer, but for your social media page. It looks at your "performance," spots what you're doing right and wrong, and gives you a clear plan — instead of just handing you a stack of confusing charts.
Who is it for?
📸
Influencers
People who post content and want more followers, more engagement, and more income.
🏪
Businesses
Brands and shops running a Facebook page who want to reach more customers and avoid PR disasters.
🧑‍💼
Agencies
Teams that manage lots of pages for lots of clients, and need results for all of them at once.
How it works — in 4 simple steps
🔗
1. Connect
You link your Facebook page to Artemis with one click. Safe and permission-based.
→
🔬
2. Analyse
Artemis reads your last 2 years of posts, comments, photos and videos behind the scenes.
→
🧠
3. Understand
It finds the hidden patterns — what your audience loves, when they're active, what's about to go viral.
→
💬
4. Advise
Art-E (our AI helper) tells you, in plain words, exactly what to post and when.
Meet Art-E — the heart of the product
🤖

Art-E is your AI guide

All the clever maths happens in the background. The user never has to read a chart if they don't want to. Instead, they just talk to Art-E — like texting a really smart friend who happens to know everything about their page.

Art-E: "Your last 3 posts did worse than usual. They went up at 9am — your audience is most active at 7pm. Try posting later today."
Art-E: "Heads up — the comments on your newest post are turning negative fast. Here's how to respond before it becomes a problem."
Art-E: "This draft you're about to post will probably do really well. Go for it! 🚀"
What makes us different
😩 Other tools
📉 Show you what already happened last week or last month.
🔢 Dump confusing numbers and charts on you and leave you to figure it out.
🧩 Treat every customer separately — they don't learn from each other.
🐌 Update slowly — once a day or once an hour.
✨ Artemis
🔮 Tells you what's going to happen — before you even post.
💬 Explains everything in plain English and tells you exactly what to do.
🌐 Gets smarter with every page that joins — everyone benefits.
⚡ Watches in real time and warns you within minutes.

Our first features — and how each one changes the game
🤖
Art-E, your AI advisor
Ask it anything about your page in normal language and it answers clearly — and always tells you what to do next.
The game-changer: other tools hand you data. Art-E hands you decisions.
🔮
Post predictions
Before you publish, Artemis estimates how well a post will do and suggests how to make it better.
The game-changer: stop guessing. Fix a weak post before it flops, not after.
🚨
Real-time alerts
A heads-up the moment something needs you — a post taking off, or comments turning nasty. Every alert comes with a fix.
The game-changer: catch a viral moment or a PR fire in minutes, not next week's report.
📰
Intelligence feed
A simple, scrollable stream of short insights about your page — each one explaining why it matters and what to do.
The game-changer: your page's "brain," readable in 10 seconds — no dashboards to decode.
🔭
Discovery
Artemis spots other pages whose audience overlaps with yours and suggests collaborations that could grow you both.
The game-changer: growth through partnership — something a chart could never suggest.
📅
Best times to post
Tells you the exact moments your specific audience is most likely to be watching.
The game-changer: tailored to your audience, not a generic "post at 9am" rule.
