How it works

Three steps, then it just runs

No dashboards to learn. Connect once and ArtemisAI starts telling you what matters, when it matters.

step 1

Connect your page

Sign in with Facebook and pick the pages you want watched. ArtemisAI reads two years of your history in the background — that's how it learns what works for your audience, not a generic one.

step 2

ArtemisAI watches

Every post, every comment, around the clock. It learns your page's rhythm: when your audience is active, what they respond to, and what a normal day looks like — so it notices when something isn't.

step 3

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Do I have to give you my Facebook password?
Never. You connect through Facebook's own official login, the same way you'd sign into any app with Facebook. You can disconnect at any time from your settings, and everything ArtemisAI holds about your page can be exported or deleted on request.
Will ArtemisAI post or reply without asking me?
No. ArtemisAI drafts — you decide. A suggested reply or a scheduled post always waits for your review. Anything automatic (like the weekly report) is something you switched on yourself, and one toggle turns it all off.
How can it predict how a post will do?
It learns from your page's own history — two years of posts and comments — and compares a new draft against what has and hasn't worked for your audience. It's guidance with the reasons attached, not a guarantee, and you can always see the numbers behind a prediction.
Is my page's data kept private?
Yes. Your data is yours: it's stored encrypted in the UK/EU, never sold, and never shown to another customer. We're registered with the ICO and you get full export and delete controls under GDPR.
When can I use it?
We're in a closed pilot with 9 UK pages now and launch publicly on 28 November 2026. If you run a page and want early access, email us — pilot places are limited but we keep a waitlist.
