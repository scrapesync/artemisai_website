01Five researched truths this whole plan stands on

Everything below follows from these. If someone challenges the plan, challenge these first — each one has a source and a number behind it.

👤
The company page is not the reach engine. People are.Company pages get ~5% of feed allocation vs ~65% for personal profiles; identical content posted by a person gets up to 561% more reach and 8x the engagement. The page is our credibility hub and archive; Asad, Saad, Jill and Hannah posting personally is the distribution.
📄
Document carousels and multi-image posts win the feed.Native PDF carousels lead every format at ~7% engagement (1.3M-post benchmark); multi-image posts deliver the most impressions for pages under 50K followers. Vertical video is rising (+36% watch time) but must hook in 2 seconds. Polls are dead post the March 2026 authenticity update.
🏠
Our audience lives on Facebook, not Discord.The community play for page owners is a free Facebook Group + a small WhatsApp circle for beta users, run with our own product visibly doing the work. LinkedIn earns B2B credibility; Facebook is where the buyers actually are.
⏳
Waitlist hype has a 30-day half-life.~50% of waitlist signups convert if launch comes within 30 days; only ~20% after 90. So we build audience now but open the referral waitlist in mid-October, six weeks out, so it is still hot on 1 December.
🐕
Our best content is the product using itself.From September, Art-E publicly runs the ArtemisAI Facebook page and we publish its calls and results weekly. The demo and the build-in-public content become the same asset — nobody can copy that story.
📊
We own a data goldmine nobody else has.99,507 real Facebook posts in the warehouse. Every "what actually drives reach" data post writes itself and positions us as the people who measured it, not guessed it.

02The six strategy pillars

🎙 Founder-led distribution

All four of us post from personal accounts 2-3x/week. Asad: build-in-public + data. Saad: design-in-public (the revamp is a content series). Jill: ops/finance of a bootstrapped AI startup. Hannah: growth experiments. The page reshares nothing bare; people reshare the page with commentary within the first hour.

Why: 8x engagement, 561% reach vs the page.

🏛 Page as credibility hub

The company page posts the polished 2-3x/week: carousels, reveals, pilot stories, event reactions. Complete profile (+30% views), all 250 monthly invite credits spent, newsletter from September. First target: 150 followers, the threshold where LinkedIn's recommendation engine kicks in.

Why: buyers check the page before they trust the person.

🎨 Design-reveal arc

Saad's revamp is a ready-made content series: audit findings, direction boards, before/after reveals of the landing page, web app and mobile app through August-September. Design content is the most shareable B2B format and it shows the product getting real.

Why: reveals = anticipation; before/after = proof.

🐕 Dogfood story

"Art-E runs our page" from September: every week we publish what the AI told us to do (slot, format, fix) and what happened, real numbers included, wins and misses. Misses build more trust than wins.

Why: the only demo competitors cannot fake or copy.

🧲 Proof engine (pilots)

Recruit 5-10 pilot pages in September. From October, one before/after story a week: "[Page] grew reach 42% in 3 weeks — here is what Art-E told them to change", screenshots included, posted with the pilot's permission so they reshare to their own audience.

Why: case studies out-persuade discounts; 30% of beta signups come from content.

🚀 Waitlist + countdown engine

Mid-October: referral waitlist opens (visible queue position, jump-the-queue referrals, 3-question qualifying survey, founding-member pricing for the first 100 pages). Late November: daily countdown, loss-framed. December: batched access, Clay-style, so feedback stays clean.

Why: Robinhood got 1M signups from these exact mechanics.

03LinkedIn algorithm rules we do not break

📅
Cadence: page 2-3x/week, founders 2-3x/week, never bursts.Weekly consistency alone drives 5.6x follower growth. Sporadic bursts get penalized by follower drop-off.
🕑
Post Tue-Thu, 10-11am or 1-2pm UK.Wednesday is the strongest day. Weekends are dead for B2B (-50-70%). Exception: founder posts at 7-8am catch decision-makers pre-work.
🔗
80% of posts carry no external link.In-body links cost 19-60% reach and the first-comment trick is patched (comment links suppressed up to 80%). When we must link, we link and accept the cost — or point at the profile/newsletter.
#️⃣
3-5 hashtags max, as labels not reach.One broad (#SocialMediaMarketing), one niche (#FacebookMarketing), one branded (#ArtemisAI). Hashtag discovery is dead; classification value remains.
📨
Newsletter from September: it bypasses the algorithm.Every edition triggers email + push + bell to 100% of subscribers (35-40% open rates) and gets Google-indexed. Biweekly "The Page Owner's Edge", repurposed from our best posts.
🤝
The first hour is a team sport.When the page posts, every team member reshares with their own added take (never a bare reshare) and comments within 60 minutes. Early engagement decides distribution.
✉️
All 250 invite credits spent every month.Each admin invites their warmest connections; credits refund on acceptance. Fastest free path to the 150-follower recommendation threshold.
💬
15 meaningful comments a day, team-wide.Commenting on our audience's and adjacent creators' posts is the cheapest awareness channel there is; thoughtful comments get profile visits.

07The hype engine: mechanics with receipts

🎟 The waitlist (opens mid-Oct)

Robinhood's exact levers, off-the-shelf tooling: visible queue position ("You are #218"), referral queue-jumping with milestone rewards at 1/3/5/10 referrals, one-click prewritten shares. Plus Superhuman's move: a 3-question qualifying survey (page size, posting frequency, biggest struggle) — filters for fit, generates segment data, and makes acceptance feel earned. Private status view, not a public leaderboard (B2B-ish audiences prefer it).

Robinhood: ~1M signups pre-launch on these mechanics.

⏱ The countdown (final week)

Countdown timers on the landing page and live in emails: a reliable 30-40% conversion lift. Loss-framed copy ("founding-member pricing disappears 15 Dec") beats gain-framed by 15-20%. The deadline and the 100-page cap are real — manufactured urgency would poison a trust product.

Cialdini scarcity, measured, with a genuine deadline.

🦉 Product Hunt (launch day, calibrated)

Worth ~2-3 team-days for the credibility assets (badge, reviews, backlinks) — not a growth channel in 2026. Playbook: 30 days of genuine PH community activity first, publish 12:01am PT, supporter waves concentrated in the first 2-6 hours (20-30 upvotes in 2 hours or no top-5), comments over upvotes, someone replying within 15 minutes for 24 hours. The waitlist email at launch minute IS our coordinated early wave.

Expectation set: 50-300 signups = a great B2B outcome.

🚪 Batched access (Clay's lesson)

Even at launch, access goes in batches: first 100 pages, then weekly admissions. Four people cannot support an unfiltered influx — the constraint becomes exclusivity, and feedback stays clean enough to act on. Beta users get 3 invites each (Arc's evangelist mechanic). Then the gate drops before Christmas: for SMB audiences, prolonged exclusivity reads as unavailability.

Clay kept the gate 15 months and 10x'd revenue after.

08Should we write blogs?

Yes — but LinkedIn-newsletter-first, SEO second. A classic blog needs 6-12 months for SEO compounding to pay, which is after launch. So: publish on the LinkedIn newsletter first (100% subscriber delivery, 35-40% opens, zero algorithm, Google-indexed anyway). From October, mirror the three strongest editions onto artemisai.co.uk/blog as SEO seeds targeting "how to grow a Facebook page", "best time to post on Facebook", "why is my facebook reach down" — queries our warehouse data answers better than anyone guessing. Every data carousel doubles as a blog section, so the marginal cost is minutes, not days. Post-launch, the blog inherits a working content machine.
