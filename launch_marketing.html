Anatomy of a post · the engagement formula
HOOK
≤10 words. Lead with a number.Number-led hooks: +35% likes. Question hooks: −34% (save questions for the end). Only ~210 chars show before "see more".
BODY
1,300-2,500 characters. Short lines. White space.+27% engagement vs short posts. 1-2 sentences per paragraph (20+ short paragraphs ≈ 1.6x reach). Max 2 emojis. First-person story beats corporate voice by 38%.
CTA
End with one specific, open question.+77% comments. Must need context to answer — never yes/no.
BANNED
"Agree?" · "Tag someone" · "Comment YES" · "Drop a 🔥"Flagged by the 2026 authenticity update: −50-80% reach, repeat offences suppress the whole account.
AFTER
Reply to every comment within 30 min.+64% total comments, 2.3x views. The golden 90 minutes decides distribution.
