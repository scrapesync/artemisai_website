QA Dashboard

Two questions, answered plainly: are the models still accurate, and did the data arrive. Plus a place to write your own SQL and get a chart out of it.

Model accuracy

One card per model we run in production. The big number is where it stands now; the line is the last 30 days. We have no human ground truth in production, so for the NLP models "accuracy" means agreement with the DeepSeek second pass, and for the rest it means the model's own calibrated confidence. Those are the honest measures available without labels. A card reading not measured means the last runs produced no second-pass comparisons to score against, which is different from scoring badly.

Sentiment: predicted vs second pass

Where the sentiment model and the second pass disagree. The diagonal is agreement; everything off it is a case worth looking at.

Routing: which tier handled the work

High-confidence predictions commit automatically; the rest escalate. If the escalation share climbs, the models are getting less sure about incoming data.

Did the data arrive?

The weekly run, table by table. Pass means it loaded inside the expected window. Anything else needs a look before the numbers on any other screen can be trusted.

Rows loaded, last 30 days

The pipeline runs weekly, so most days are zero by design. The spikes are the runs, and a missing spike is the thing to notice.

Quality checks

Completeness is the share of rows that actually carry a label. The rest count problems, where zero is the goal.

Build your own chart

Write a read-only SELECT against Redshift and turn the result into a chart or a table. The first column is the x-axis (or the label); every numeric column after it gets plotted. Results cap at 5,000 rows, queries time out at 20 seconds, and nothing but SELECT will run.
