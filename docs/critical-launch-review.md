## What should not consume automatic launch effort

1. **Untriggered fundraising applications:** retain the record, execute only for a confirmed funding need.
2. **Unselected marketing channels:** keep positioning, launch copy and customer outreach; Product Hunt/directory-specific assets depend on a channel decision and working access.
3. **Duplicate records or reports:** one pilot roster, one cost model, one evidence index. Update changed facts at later checkpoints.
4. **Premature certification:** index real evidence; do not start a SOC 2 project merely because a ticket mentioned “SOC2-later”.
5. **Assumed purchases and bookings:** assess EU representation and external testing needs, then confirm applicability/resources. Do not count an unbooked provider as available capacity.
6. **Rebuilding completed NLP:** preserve completion and the existing QA/NLP trackers. Remaining integration, data freshness and verified quality defects have different outputs.
7. **Unsupported incident response:** the two disputed incident-specific assignments stay excluded. Preventive testing and rehearsal are future preparedness, not claims that an attack occurred. Social-media crisis examples concern customers' reputations and are separate from security incidents.

Conditional records remain visible and included in conservative capacity totals. No savings have been fabricated from conditional wording, merging reports or lowering estimates.
